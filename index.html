Meet your doctor

A true ally in your care

Dr. Maria Flora G. Trimor-Tamoria is a dedicated physician specializing in primary care, with more than 22 years in practice and a special interest in geriatrics. With a passion for holistic patient care, she has expanded her expertise to include aesthetics, wellness and function, offering a comprehensive approach to health and beauty.

Dr. Trimor-Tamoria is committed to helping her patients look, feel and function their best, integrating medical expertise with the latest advancements in aesthetic and wellness treatments. She takes pride in a compassionate, thorough and professional approach, and cares for every patient as if they were family.

She is proud to serve the National City and San Diego area, bringing high-quality, patient-centered care to her community.

  • Primary careCare for adults 18 and older, from check-ups to long-term conditions
  • GeriatricsAttentive care for older adults and their caregivers
  • Wellness & functionWeight loss, pain relief and recovery to help you move and feel better
  • Laser aestheticsSkin and body treatments planned with your full health picture in mind
